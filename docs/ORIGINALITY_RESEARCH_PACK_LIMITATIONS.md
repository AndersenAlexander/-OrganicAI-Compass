# Originality Research Pack Limitations

Implemented behavior is deterministic and suitable for MVP evaluation, but it is not scientific validation.

Known limitations:

- No production Alembic migration chain exists in the repository.
- Scenario candidate generation is intentionally constrained to active profile context and curated roles.
- Market signals are date-bound and coverage-limited.
- Robustness metrics vary selected non-sensitive variables and do not prove correctness.
- Synthetic fairness audits can find rule-level risks, but do not prove real-world fairness.
- No protected attributes are inferred for real users.
- Roadmap proposals are not inserted automatically.
- Evidence Passport changes remain under the existing evidence workflow.
- The Recommendation System Card must be reviewed when scoring versions change.

Deferred work:

- Production migrations.
- Admin review workflow for fairness fixture maintenance.
- Larger scenario comparison history.
- Exportable PDF report.
- Empirical research analysis after participant consent and data collection.
