# Week 7 thesis claim consistency note

Date: 2026-08-20

This note is a claim boundary for the existing thesis support material. It does not replace or rewrite the thesis.

## Safe claims for Chapter 4 — implementation

Chapter 4 may state that the prototype implements:

- a progressive Human Diagnostic and editable Human Potential Map;
- separate Natural Fit, Capability Fit, Evidence Strength and Transition Feasibility dimensions;
- evidence gaps, experiments, provisional evidence proposals and explicit recalibration;
- market/application and interview workflows with provenance and confirmation boundaries;
- adviser collaboration, browser capture, Decision Journal and Career Encyclopedia;
- deterministic Adaptive Evidence-Gain, Pareto transition simulation, robustness analysis and synthetic fairness fixtures;
- a Recommendation System Card with limitations, oversight and provenance;
- local Demo Mode, provider fallbacks and privacy/ownership boundaries.

These are implementation claims supported by source inspection, migrations, automated tests and local UAT flows.

## Safe claims for Chapter 5 — technical evaluation

Chapter 5 may conclude that the documented local artifact:

- passed the Week 7 backend regression: `215 passed, 5 skipped`;
- passed frontend unit tests: `54 passed`;
- passed frontend-only Playwright regression: `144 passed`;
- passed the focused accessibility visual smoke: `1 passed`;
- passed typecheck, production build, browser-extension build and Python compilation;
- passed fresh Alembic upgrades through `0009_collaboration_traceability_extensions`;
- preserved ownership, privacy, explicit mutation, evidence calibration and provider-fallback boundaries under tested fixtures.

Use “technical validation”, “automated regression”, “deterministic fixture validation” or “local UAT evidence”.

## Claims that remain pending

The following require participant or operational evidence and must remain pending:

- empirical participant evaluation: `PENDING / NOT EXECUTED`;
- usability, trust calibration, perceived agency and relevance outcomes;
- statistically demonstrated improvement or superiority;
- longitudinal career outcomes or employability effects;
- live NAV, voice, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, email, cloud and production PostgreSQL operations;
- legal/privacy approval, penetration testing and public production monitoring.

## Prohibited wording

Do not write that the system:

- improves career outcomes;
- is scientifically or psychometrically validated;
- is fair or fairness-certified;
- predicts hiring or employability;
- demonstrates statistically significant usability or trust improvement;
- is public-production ready.

## Classification vocabulary

| Label | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `IMPLEMENTED` | Present in the code and documented. |
| `AUTOMATED-TESTED` | Covered by a repeatable automated test. |
| `MANUALLY-VALIDATED` | Checked in a local UI/UAT or source audit. |
| `DEMO-ONLY` | Deterministic fictional fixture, not participant evidence. |
| `PROVIDER-DEPENDENT` | Requires credentials, external service or operational setup. |
| `EMPIRICALLY-VALIDATED` | Not currently used; no participant study has completed. |
| `NOT YET VALIDATED` | Requires future operational or empirical evidence. |
