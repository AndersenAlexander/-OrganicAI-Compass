# DPIA Screening

Technical draft - requires legal review before public deployment.

Potential higher-risk areas:

- AI-generated career guidance and profiling.
- Voice interaction and transcription.
- Research participation and pseudonymous analytics.
- External AI provider processing.
- Account deletion across backups and provider systems.

Current mitigations:

- Privacy Center controls.
- Ephemeral conversation and voice modes.
- Research disabled by default.
- Voice audio storage disabled.
- Secret fields excluded from export.
- Recent authentication for sensitive actions.
- Deletion suppression ledger for restore workflows.

Decision: DPIA/legal review is required before public deployment.
