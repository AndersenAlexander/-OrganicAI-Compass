# Career Encyclopedia

Career Encyclopedia is a deterministic, curated reference catalogue separate from Market Radar. The UI labels it `CURATED REFERENCE` and `NOT LIVE MARKET DATA`.

The catalogue contains 16 detailed profiles across four families: AI and software, Design and product, Consulting and strategy, and Learning and communication. Profiles expose responsibilities, skills, tools, entry routes, transferable skills, portfolio evidence, adjacent roles, AI transformation notes, limitations, source metadata, review date and version.

Role title/alias search, family filtering and skill-keyword filtering are deterministic. Encyclopedia content may inform an explicitly saved hypothesis or experiment, but it is not user evidence and does not assert current demand, salary or live vacancies.
