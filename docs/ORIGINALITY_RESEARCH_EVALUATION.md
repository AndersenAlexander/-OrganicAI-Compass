# Originality Research Evaluation

Potential primary research question:

"Does adaptive, evidence-gain-based career experimentation combined with multi-objective transition comparison provide more actionable and better-calibrated career guidance than conventional assessment-based recommendations?"

Potential sub-questions:

1. Does adaptive experiment selection reduce unresolved career uncertainty?
2. Does the selected experiment produce more relevant evidence than a manually selected generic activity?
3. Does Pareto-based path comparison improve understanding of trade-offs?
4. Does robustness information improve trust calibration?
5. Does the system help users identify which assumptions materially affect a recommendation?

Implemented research support:

- Consent-gated originality research sessions.
- Baseline and experimental workflow records.
- Feedback capture.
- Pseudonymous ID.
- Export filtering that excludes raw journal text and raw transcripts.

No empirical results are claimed before data collection and analysis.
