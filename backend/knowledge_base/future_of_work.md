# Future of Work

The future of work is shaped by automation, augmentation, and new forms of collaboration. Repetitive tasks may be automated, while human strengths such as empathy, strategic judgment, creativity, leadership, and ethical responsibility become more important.

## Human Advantage

People create value by framing problems, understanding context, building trust, making responsible decisions, and connecting technical possibilities to human needs. AI can support research, drafting, analysis, simulation, and learning, but humans remain accountable for direction and impact.

## Career Adaptation

A useful adaptation plan includes learning AI basics, identifying tasks that can be augmented, strengthening communication skills, and building small projects that show practical human-AI collaboration.
