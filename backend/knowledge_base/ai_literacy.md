# AI Literacy

AI literacy means understanding what artificial intelligence can do, where it is limited, and how to use it responsibly. For OrganicAI Compass, AI is treated as an assistant for thinking, drafting, comparing options, and learning. It is not a replacement for human judgment, values, care, or accountability.

## Practical Guidance

Start with simple questions, ask for examples, compare multiple outputs, and verify important facts. A strong human-AI workflow keeps the person in charge of goals, context, ethics, and final decisions. AI is most useful when the user can inspect, challenge, and improve the output.

## Boundaries

AI systems can produce errors, incomplete context, or confident-sounding claims. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal data and should verify legal, medical, financial, and safety-critical information with qualified sources.
