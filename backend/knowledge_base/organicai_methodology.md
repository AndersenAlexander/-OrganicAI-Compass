# OrganicAI Methodology

OrganicAI Compass uses a human-centred methodology for moving from fear to clarity, agency, creativity, and contribution. The platform combines diagnostic reflection, AI literacy, fear transformation, coaching, and roadmap generation.

## Fear to Creativity

The method separates what is real, what is uncertain, what the user can control, and what creative action can be taken. This reduces anxiety by turning abstract fear into concrete learning and contribution.

## Roadmap

The roadmap moves through short, medium, and long horizons: 7 days for alignment, 30 days for skill and project momentum, and 6 months for contribution and sustainable growth.
