# Robotics Awareness

Robotics combines sensors, software, mechanical systems, and automation. Robots can perform physical tasks, assist people, improve safety, and support repetitive work, but they also raise questions about jobs, responsibility, accessibility, and social impact.

## Human-Centred Robotics

Human-centred robotics designs machines around human wellbeing, safety, dignity, and usefulness. Robots should support people, reduce harmful work, and respect human control in sensitive settings.

## Learning Path

A beginner can start by learning sensors, actuators, basic automation logic, safety principles, and examples of collaborative robots in healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, and education.
