# Career Resilience Methodology

Retrieval filters: career_guidance, responsible_career_recommendations, evidence_based_skills, career_transition.

OrganicAI Compass treats a career recommendation as a hypothesis, not a deterministic conclusion. A recommendation should use language such as "Current evidence suggests..." and "This career direction remains a hypothesis." It should not say that a role is ideal, guaranteed, psychologically proven, or professionally certain.

The resilience workflow is:

1. Assessment
2. Career hypothesis
3. Career experiment
4. Evidence collection
5. Career recommendation recalibration
6. Market and support feasibility
7. Best supported career path
8. User-confirmed roadmap

Role experiments are small practical simulations. They are not exams, certifications, employment decisions, or automated hiring assessments. Their purpose is to generate structured evidence before the user invests heavily in reskilling.

Rubric scores are deterministic. AI may explain results, summarise strengths, and suggest reflective questions, but it must not calculate or alter scores, invent evidence, certify competence, or claim the user is objectively suitable for employment.

Evidence Passport entries separate self-report from practical, external, and professional evidence. Course completion alone records learning exposure and does not create practical verification.
