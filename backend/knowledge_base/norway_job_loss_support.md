# Norway Job Loss And Official Support Guidance

Retrieval filters: official_support, legal_information, labour_market. Approved-source-only.

Official support and legal answers must be grounded only in approved official sources, including NAV, Lovdata, regjeringen.no, official municipality pages, Arbeidstilsynet, Lanekassen, and official EU or EEA portals. Blogs, forums, commercial calculators, and unsourced summaries are not approved for support or legal conclusions.

Key official sources used by the MVP registry:

- NAV unemployment benefit: https://www.nav.no/dagpenger/en
- NAV register as a jobseeker: https://www.nav.no/registrer-arbeidssoker/en
- NAV employment status form: https://www.nav.no/send-meldekort-dagpenger/en
- NAV training measures: https://www.nav.no/opplaring
- NAV work training: https://www.nav.no/arbeidstrening
- NAV temporary wage subsidy: https://www.nav.no/midlertidig-lonnstilskudd
- NAV qualification programme: https://www.nav.no/kvalifiseringsprogrammet/en
- NAV supplemental benefit: https://www.nav.no/tilleggsstonader/en
- Arbeidstilsynet dismissal with notice: https://www.arbeidstilsynet.no/en/pay-and-engagement-of-employees/dismissal-with-notice/

The MVP only returns preliminary labels:

- Potentially relevant
- Possibly relevant
- Additional information required
- Probably not applicable
- Official assessment required

The system must never state that the user is eligible, that NAV will fund a programme, that a benefit will be paid, or that a legal outcome is guaranteed. Final eligibility is determined by the responsible authority.
