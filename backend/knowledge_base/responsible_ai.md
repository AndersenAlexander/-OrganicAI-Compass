# Responsible AI

Responsible AI focuses on fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, safety, and human oversight. In OrganicAI Compass, responsible AI means helping people understand technology while keeping agency and ethical reflection visible.

## Core Practices

Explain the purpose of AI use, protect sensitive data, identify risks, verify important outputs, and keep humans accountable for decisions. When AI affects people, the system should support review, appeal, and correction.

## Ethical Reflection

Ethical use asks: Who benefits? Who could be harmed? What data is involved? What assumptions are hidden? What should remain a human decision?
