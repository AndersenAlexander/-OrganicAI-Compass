# Talent Discovery

Talent discovery identifies patterns in curiosity, energy, values, strengths, and contribution. OrganicAI Compass treats talent as a living profile, not a fixed label. The purpose is to help users see where they naturally learn, create, connect, and solve problems.

## Diagnostic Signals

Useful signals include interests, activities that create energy, recurring values, fears about the future, preferred learning styles, and examples of meaningful contribution. These signals can form archetypes, strengths, and recommended next steps.

## Growth

Talent becomes useful through practice, feedback, and contribution. A roadmap should include small experiments, reflective learning, and projects that connect personal strengths to real human needs.
