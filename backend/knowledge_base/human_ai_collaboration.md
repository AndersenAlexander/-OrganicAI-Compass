# Human-AI Collaboration

Human-AI collaboration is the practice of combining machine speed and pattern recognition with human values, intention, responsibility, and creativity. The goal is not to compete with AI, but to design workflows where AI amplifies human potential.

## Collaboration Patterns

Useful patterns include AI as mirror, co-creator, research assistant, tutor, critique partner, and builder assistant. Each pattern requires clear human intent, review, and iteration. Trust grows when teams document when AI is used, what sources support the answer, and what decisions remain human-owned.

## Team Trust

Teams build trust by starting with education, defining acceptable use, protecting data, involving people early, and measuring outcomes transparently. AI adoption should increase agency rather than create surveillance or pressure.
